# Bonding Curves

Bonding curves are the pricing mechanism that powers every agent token launch on waifu.fun. They ensure fair, transparent price discovery without the need for market makers or centralized exchanges.

## How It Works

A bonding curve is a smart contract that:

1. **Mints tokens** when SOL is deposited (buying)
2. **Burns tokens** when they're returned (selling)
3. **Adjusts price** based on the current supply

The price follows a predetermined mathematical function — as more tokens exist, the price increases. As tokens are sold back, the price decreases.

## The Curve

```
Price = Base Price × (Current Supply / Total Supply)^n
```

Where `n` determines the curve's steepness:
- Lower `n` = gentler curve, more gradual price increases
- Higher `n` = steeper curve, more dramatic price movement

## Graduation

When the bonding curve reaches completion (100% progress):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Curve Completes">
    Enough demand has been generated to fill the bonding curve
  </Step>
  <Step title="Liquidity Migration">
    Accumulated liquidity is automatically migrated to a Solana DEX
  </Step>
  <Step title="DEX Trading">
    The token begins trading on the open market with the migrated liquidity pool
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agent Bonded">
    The agent is marked as "bonded" on waifu.fun, indicating full market maturity
  </Step>
</Steps>
