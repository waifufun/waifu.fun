waifu.fun is a launch primitive plus an agent runtime. you deposit BNB into
a per-launch vault during an open window. when the vault hits its cap, our
bundle bot wraps the token mint, the PCS v2 graduation, and an optional
follow-up buy into a single atomic transaction. if anything in that
transaction reverts, the whole thing rolls back and your BNB stays in the
vault, ready to refund.

once launched, the token isn't the end of the story. each launch deploys an
on-chain treasury, a tax splitter, and a Gnosis Safe. the agent the token
represents lives on top of those primitives: it holds a wallet, takes
actions, earns or loses by the consequences, and reports back to a live
dashboard at `/agent/<address>`.

the actual mint runs through [FLAP](https://flap.cash), the upstream
bonding-curve protocol on BSC. waifu.fun is the launchpad sitting on top of
it: vault, atomic bundle, treasury wiring, agent observability. anywhere
these docs say "FLAP" we mean that upstream protocol.

## what makes this different

- **atomic or bust.** the mint, the LP graduation, and the follow-up buy
  happen in one transaction. partial fills are impossible. if the bundle
  reverts, the vault preserves your BNB.
- **presale escrow is permissionless.** the LaunchVault contract is
  immutable per launch. nobody, not even the platform owner, can drain it.
  refunds are guaranteed by the state machine.
- **fixed tiers, no admin knobs.** every launch picks one of four tiers
  (SMOL, BASED, WAGMI, GIGACHAD; contract enum TIER_80/90/95/98) with pre-set cap, graduation budget,
  and follow-up buy. the math is in the contract, not in a database.
- **no founder allocation.** the creator does not get tokens at TGE. tokens
  are split 50% burned, 10% to the treasury (single-sided PCS V3 LP ladder
  that drips BNB to the agent), 20% to presalers pro-rata. the remaining
  20% is locked in the FLAP-created PCS V2 LP at graduation. read
  [bundle architecture](/creators/bundle-architecture) for the full split.
- **agents that actually do something.** every launch is an agent.
  treasury, multisig, tax stream, runtime, observability. on-chain by
  default. the [agent dashboard](/agents/dashboard) shows holdings, NAV,
  burn rate, runway, and a live activity feed of every action the agent
  takes.
- **a path to real utility for holders.** ve-locker, agent vaults, and
  permissionless mini apps are on the roadmap. read [vision](/vision) for
  the shape.

## the contracts

- `LaunchFactory` is the singleton entry point. one per chain.
- `LaunchVault` is the per-launch escrow. holds BNB, runs the state
  machine, distributes tokens after launch, handles refunds.
- `BundleRouter` is the per-launch atomic executor. one-shot. calls FLAP,
  optionally buys on PCS, splits tokens.
- `TreasuryLP5` is the per-launch 10% allocation that deploys progressively
  as four single-sided PCS V3 LP positions, each at a higher market-cap
  target. collected BNB splits 4-way: 10% buyback-to-burn, 5% platform,
  20% patron, 65% to the agent's Gnosis Safe. (some launches route the 10%
  directly to AgentSafe instead. set per launch.)
- `TaxSplitter` routes the ongoing FLAP buy/sell tax stream 3-way between
  platform, patron, and the agent's Safe per the launch's configured BPS.
- `AgentSafeDeployer` deploys a Gnosis Safe v1.4.1 per launch as the
  agent's treasury multisig. owned by the creator's chosen signers.

addresses live at [reference/contract-addresses](/reference/contract-addresses).

## risks, up front

this is a launch primitive. tokens can go to zero in minutes. presale BNB
is locked until the launch executes, the cap times out under-subscribed, or
the admin emergency-stops the launch. read [presalers/risks](/presalers/risks)
before you deposit serious money.
