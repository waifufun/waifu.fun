waifu.fun is a launch primitive. you deposit BNB into a per-launch vault during
an open window. when the vault hits its cap, our bundle bot wraps the FLAP mint,
the PCS v2 graduation, and an optional follow-up buy into a single atomic
transaction. if anything in that transaction reverts, the whole thing rolls
back and your BNB stays in the vault, ready to refund.

that is the whole shape of the platform. everything in these docs is a detail
underneath that shape.

## what makes this different

- **atomic or bust.** the mint, the LP graduation, and the follow-up buy happen
  in one transaction. partial fills are impossible. if the bundle reverts, the
  vault preserves your BNB.
- **presale escrow is permissionless.** the LaunchVault contract is immutable
  per launch. nobody, not even the platform owner, can drain it. refunds are
  guaranteed by the state machine.
- **fixed tiers, no admin knobs.** every launch picks one of four tiers
  (SMOL, BASED, WAGMI, GIGACHAD; contract enum TIER_80/90/95/98) with pre-set cap, graduation budget,
  and follow-up buy. the math is in the contract, not in a database.
- **no founder allocation.** the creator does not get tokens at TGE. tokens
  are split 50% burned, 10% to TREASURY_LP, ~40% to presalers pro-rata. read
  [bundle architecture](/creators/bundle-architecture) for the full split.

## the contracts

- `LaunchFactory` is the singleton entry point. one per chain.
- `LaunchVault` is the per-launch escrow. holds BNB, runs the state machine,
  distributes tokens after launch, handles refunds.
- `BundleRouter` is the per-launch atomic executor. one-shot. calls FLAP,
  optionally buys on PCS, splits tokens.
- `TreasuryLP` is the per-launch 10% custody vault. future waves will deploy
  managed LP from here.

addresses live at [reference/contract-addresses](/reference/contract-addresses).

## risks, up front

this is a launch primitive. tokens can go to zero in minutes. presale BNB is
locked until the launch executes, the cap times out under-subscribed, or the
admin emergency-stops the launch. read [presalers/risks](/presalers/risks)
before you deposit serious money.
