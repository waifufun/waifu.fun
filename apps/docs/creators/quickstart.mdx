---
title: "quickstart"
description: "launch a token on waifu.fun in five steps"
---

you need a BSC wallet, some BNB for the creation fee, and a token concept.
everything else the platform handles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="sign in">
    go to [waifu.fun](https://waifu.fun) and connect your wallet. you will be
    asked to sign a SIWE message. this proves wallet ownership without spending
    gas. SIWE binds your session to a single address on BSC mainnet (chain id 56).
  </Step>
  <Step title="pick a tier">
    four options:
    - **TIER_80** curve-only, no PCS LP, lower-stakes launch
    - **TIER_90** 32 BNB cap, 20 BNB graduation, 12 BNB follow-up buy
    - **TIER_95** 64 BNB cap, 20 BNB graduation, 44 BNB follow-up buy
    - **TIER_98** 160 BNB cap, 20 BNB graduation, 140 BNB follow-up buy

    pick the cap you think your community can actually hit. an under-subscribed
    vault refunds at the deadline. see [choose your tier](/creators/choose-your-tier).
  </Step>
  <Step title="write metadata">
    name, symbol, description, image, optional social links. the platform
    uploads to IPFS and pins it. the IPFS CID becomes part of your
    `NewTokenV6` params on-chain. see [write your metadata](/creators/write-your-metadata).
  </Step>
  <Step title="deploy the launch">
    the platform calls `LaunchFactory.createLaunch(config)`. this deploys three
    contracts: `LaunchVault`, `BundleRouter`, `TreasuryLP`. you pay the gas plus
    the platform creation fee.

    once deployed, your launch is in `OPEN` state. anyone can deposit BNB into
    the vault. you do not need to be online for the rest. the bundle bot
    handles execution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="watch the cap fill">
    the dashboard shows live deposits. when the cap is hit (or the window
    closes with the cap met), the bundle bot picks up the launch and runs
    `executeBundle()`. you will see a `BundleExecuted` event on the router,
    a PCS pair on the FLAP token, and tokens flowing to claimers.

    if the cap is not hit by the deadline, the vault automatically goes to
    `REFUND` and depositors get their BNB plus a pro-rata bonus share.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## what you do not have to do

- write any code
- run a bot
- hold custody of presaler BNB
- send tokens manually after launch (claims are pull-based)
- maintain a multisig (the contracts are immutable per launch)

## what you should do

- announce the launch with the vault address so depositors know where to send BNB
- engage with the community during the OPEN window
- after launch, manage the 10% TreasuryLP balance through the platform UI
  (future feature, see [after launch](/creators/after-launch))
